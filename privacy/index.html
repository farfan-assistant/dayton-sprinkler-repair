Privacy Policy

Dayton Sprinkler Repair Quotes is a lead-matching website for sprinkler and irrigation repair requests in the Dayton, Ohio area.

What we collect

If you submit a quote request, the site may collect your name, email, phone number, ZIP/city, repair details, timeline, and consent to be contacted about the request.

How information is used

Information may be used to respond to your request, evaluate whether a local provider may be a fit, route the request to an independent provider when available, prevent spam, and measure site performance.

Form processing and spam prevention

Form submissions are processed through FormSubmit so the request can be delivered by email. The form uses a hidden honeypot field and spam keyword filtering to reduce automated submissions.

Sharing

Repair request details may be shared with an independent local sprinkler or irrigation provider only so they can respond to your request. We do not sell unrelated marketing lists.

Analytics and tracking

The site may use analytics, Search Console, form conversion tracking, or call tracking after approval. Tracking should be documented in the active measurement plan.

Important disclosure

This site is a lead-matching website. It does not perform sprinkler or irrigation repair, does not claim a local office, and does not guarantee provider availability, pricing, licensing, insurance, or work quality.
